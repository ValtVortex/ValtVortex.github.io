Modified APKs

Unverified third-party app source

⌄
  • JollyMod ↗

JollyMod presents itself as a MOD APK site. Modified APKs can tamper with code, permissions, ads, subscriptions, or account flows; they may violate app terms or intellectual-property rights and can expose your device or accounts to risk. Prefer the original developer, Google Play, or a trusted official F-Droid repository. Never assume a site's “tested” or “safe” badge is independent verification.
